Before you visit

What to bring for each job.

Pick a service to see the papers usually needed. Carry originals plus one photocopy where you have it.

Please note: new Aadhaar enrolment, Aadhaar detail updates and Aadhaar–mobile linking are not done at this centre. Only printing of the Aadhaar card is available here.

Aadhaar card printing

आधार कार्ड प्रिंट
  • A clear copy of the Aadhaar — downloaded e-Aadhaar PDF, mAadhaar screen, or the printed card itself
  • Only the Aadhaar number? We can pull up the details and print from that
  • For a family, send every Aadhaar together and collect them in one visit
  • Print available on paper or on a hard PVC card, your choice

Not available here: new Aadhaar enrolment, Aadhaar detail corrections / updates and Aadhaar–mobile linking. This centre prints the Aadhaar card only — for enrolment or updates, please visit the Aadhaar Seva Kendra or your bank.

PAN card

पैन कार्ड
  • Aadhaar card (PAN is now issued through Aadhaar e-KYC)
  • Mobile number linked with Aadhaar for the OTP
  • For a correction or reprint, a copy of the old PAN card
  • For a firm or shop, the partnership deed or registration proof

The e-PAN arrives by email; we can print the physical PVC card as well.

Passport & police verification (PCC)

पासपोर्ट एवं पुलिस क्लियरेंस
  • Aadhaar card and one address proof
  • Birth certificate, or matriculation certificate for date of birth
  • Bank passbook or cancelled cheque, if asked for
  • Old passport, for a reissue

Appointment slot and police verification are handled by the passport office; we file and track the application.

Ayushman Bharat card

आयुष्मान कार्ड — 70 वर्ष से अधिक सभी नागरिक
  • Aadhaar card of the applicant
  • A mobile number for the OTP and the e-card
  • For a family card, the Aadhaar of each member

There is no charge for the card itself under the scheme. Carry the Aadhaar of the senior citizen.

Income, caste, residence, EWS & OBC (NCL) certificates

आय, जाति, निवास, ईडब्ल्यूएस, ओबीसी प्रमाण पत्र
  • Aadhaar card and a passport-size photo
  • Residence proof — ration card, electricity bill or land paper
  • Old certificate, if you are renewing one
  • For EWS / OBC (NCL), the family income proof and caste details

You get the acknowledgement the same day; approval is done by the block / circle office.

Do I have to come in person?

For Aadhaar printing, no — send the e-copy on WhatsApp and anyone can collect it. For work that needs the applicant's signature, photo or verification — PAN, certificates, pension, Ayushman — the applicant should come. Aadhaar enrolment and updates are not done here at all.

How long does a certificate take?

The application is filed the same day and you get the acknowledgement before you leave. Approval and issue are done by the block or circle office, so the final certificate reaches you after their processing.

What will it cost?

Government portal fees are charged as they are, and our service charge depends on the form. Call or WhatsApp the details first and we will tell you the total before you come.

Can I send the documents on WhatsApp?

Yes, for a first check — send a clear photo or PDF and we will tell you what is missing. For most services the original or a printed copy is needed at the counter.

Is the Ayushman card free for senior citizens?

There is no charge for the card itself under PM-JAY for citizens aged 70 and above. Carry an Aadhaar card and a mobile number for the OTP.

Which cards can be printed on PVC?

Aadhaar, PAN, DL, RC / Owner Book, EPIC, ABC ID, ABHA, e-Shram and similar cards. A clear e-copy on your phone is usually enough — we check the print with you before handing it over.
